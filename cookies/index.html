Cookie Policy

Exactly what gets set, by whom, for how long, and how to switch it off.

Effective 27 October 2026 · Last updated 27 October 2026

1. What this covers

Cookies are small files a site stores in your browser. This policy covers cookies and similar technologies — pixels, local storage and SDKs — used on vayoo.store. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy.

2. Nothing non-essential loads until you say so

When you first arrive, analytics and advertising are off. No Google Ads tag and no Meta Pixel is loaded, and no identifier is set for them, until you make a choice. This is not a notice telling you cookies are already running — they are not.

We use Google Consent Mode v2, which is set to deny advertising and analytics storage by default and only switches to granted if you accept.

5. Changing your mind

Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, which the DPDP Act requires.

  • Open cookie settings from here or from the footer of any page, and switch categories off.
  • Clear your browser's cookies and site data for vayoo.store, which resets everything including your consent record.
  • Use your browser's own controls to block cookies generally — every major browser has them under Settings, Privacy.
  • Opt out directly with the providers: Google ad settings and Meta ad preferences.

Turning advertising off does not remove any feature of this site.

6. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

There is no agreed standard for how sites should respond to Do Not Track, so we do not rely on it. Because nothing non-essential loads without your explicit consent, the practical outcome for a DNT or GPC user is the same: no advertising or analytics cookies unless you opt in here.
