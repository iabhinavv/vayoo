4. Advertising, Google Ads and the Meta Pixel

We intend to advertise Vayoo on Google and on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram). If — and only if — you accept advertising cookies, the following run on this site:

  • Google Ads and Google Analytics 4, operated by Google LLC and Google India Private Limited. These set cookies that let us measure which ads lead to visits, and let Google show you Vayoo ads on other websites and apps you visit. This is called remarketing.
  • Meta Pixel, operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. and Meta Platforms Ireland Limited. This reports your actions on this site back to Meta so we can measure ad performance and build audiences, including showing Vayoo ads to you and to people Meta considers similar to you.

Third-party vendors, Google included, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website. These vendors may combine what they collect here with data they hold about you from elsewhere. We do not control that processing and we do not receive your identity from them — we see aggregate reports.

You can refuse all of it and still use this site in full. Nothing on vayoo.store is withheld from people who decline advertising cookies.

To opt out independently of us:

  • Google ad personalisation: adssettings.google.com
  • Google Analytics browser add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Meta ad preferences: facebook.com/adpreferences
  • Or simply withdraw consent here, which stops the tags loading on this site.

5. Consent, and how to withdraw it

Where we rely on consent, that consent is free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, given by a clear affirmative action, as DPDP s.6 requires. We do not use pre-ticked boxes and we do not treat continued browsing as agreement.

Withdrawing is as easy as giving. Use the cookie settings link in the footer of every page, or write to privacy@vayoo.store. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out before you withdrew, and we will stop the relevant processing within a reasonable period of your request.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and what we do with it
  • Correct, complete, update or erase your personal data
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated
  • Complain to us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if we do not resolve it

Write to privacy@vayoo.store and we will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, so that we do not hand your data to someone else. These rights are free to exercise.

You also have duties under DPDP s.15 — in particular, not to impersonate anyone else or file a false or frivolous complaint.

8. Children and young people

This site is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without verifiable parental consent, and under DPDP s.9 we do not carry out behavioural monitoring, profiling or targeted advertising directed at children — the advertising audiences we build exclude under-18s.

If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to privacy@vayoo.store and we will delete it.

9. Security

We apply reasonable security safeguards appropriate to the small volume of data we hold: access limited to the founders and staff who need it, multi-factor authentication on the accounts that hold personal data, encryption in transit (HTTPS across the whole site), and reliance on providers who encrypt at rest.

No safeguard is perfect. If a personal data breach occurs we will notify each affected Data Principal and the Data Protection Board of India in the form and within the time the DPDP Rules require.
