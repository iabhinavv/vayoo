Our story

Clean air, for the people breathing the worst of it

The best time to make a change was yesterday. The second best time is now.

Our mission

To become India’s number one clean-tech appliances brand, and help India become cleaner and fresher in the process.

Our vision

To give India the birth-right of pure air to breathe, and build a better tomorrow for the generation after this one.

people

What we are solving, and for whom

The crisis of unbreathable air in India’s metros — for the mass majority of youth, elderly and children who cannot afford an expensive purifier but deserve fresh air exactly as much as anyone who can.

clock

Why now, and why us

We came to this from the inside. The founding team grew up in cities where AQI was never a word anyone used, and met the problem head-on when work moved them to Delhi NCR.

rupee

Our USP

Being the brand that is there for the masses — affordable, with no compromise on quality. Because not just you, but everyone deserves to breathe better.

heart

How we build

Lean supply chains, a small team, and a refusal to publish a specification we have not measured. If a number is not on this site yet, it is because we do not have it confirmed — not because it is inconvenient.
