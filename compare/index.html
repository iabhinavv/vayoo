Choosing

Which one is actually yours?

Pick the C-10 if the problem is your commute

You spend forty minutes a day sealed in a cabin behind a diesel exhaust. It is the smallest volume of air you breathe and the dirtiest, which makes it the cheapest problem on this page to fix.

Pick the D-10 if the problem is where you work

A desk, a cabin, a study table. You are stationary for nine hours and you want something quiet enough to forget about.

Pick the R-10 if the problem is where you sleep

A bedroom or a nursery, and you want evidence rather than a promise. It is the only one of the three with a meter on the front, which is also why it carries the stronger guarantee.

Pick two if you have two rooms

One unit does one closed room. We would rather say that plainly than let you buy a single R-10 for a three-bedroom flat and feel cheated in November.
