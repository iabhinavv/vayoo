Why it costs this little

The filter is not the expensive part.

An H13 filter is a graded, manufactured commodity. What tends to sit on top of it is an app, an account, a subscription, a companion cloud service and a Wi-Fi chip that will be unsupported in four years. We left all of that out and spent the money on the filter.

  • No app, no account — plug it in and press the button. That is the whole setup.
  • No subscription — no filter club, no auto-ship you have to remember to cancel.
  • Nothing to go obsolete — there is no cloud service to switch off in 2031.
  • The same H13 in all three — the ₹1,800 C-10 filters to the same grade as the ₹6,500 R-10. You are paying for airflow and coverage, never for a better filter.
