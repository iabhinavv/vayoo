The Catastrophe of Air Pollution

Air pollution's effect cannot be understated. It impacts life expectancy, increases vulnerability to lung and heart diseases, and affects overall cognitive function.

Household & Vehicular

Diesel vehicles are a major source of black carbon. Open waste burning adds dioxins and heavy metals.

Agricultural & Deforestation

Crop burning, pesticides, and fertilizers. Loss of forests reduces Earth's natural purification.

Industrial & Construction

Factories, power plants, and poor waste disposal lead to asbestos and chemical pollution.
