FAQ

Air purifier questions, answered straight

Including the ones where the honest answer is “no” or “we have not measured that yet”.

Do air purifiers actually work in Indian conditions?

In a closed room, yes. The clearest published measurement we know of is a Delhi office in January 2026, where the indoor reading was 309 against 348 outside with no purifier running, and 48 with one running. What a purifier cannot do is help with a window open, or clean the air outside your door.

What does HEPA H13 mean?

HEPA filters are graded. H13 is the first grade classed as medical-grade and must capture 99.95% of particles at 0.3 microns — the hardest particle size to trap. Grades below it (H11, H12) catch measurably less.

Is “HEPA-type” the same thing?

No, and the distinction matters more than almost anything else on a listing. “HEPA-type”, “HEPA-like” and “HEPA-style” specifically mean the filter did not meet the HEPA standard. They are usually H11, around 95% capture.

Which Vayoo model do I need?

Measure the room in feet and multiply length by breadth. Up to about 70 sq.ft. or a car cabin, the C-10. Between 80 and 125 sq.ft., the D-10. From there up to 400 sq.ft., the R-10. If you fall between two, take the larger.

Can one unit cover my whole flat?

No. One unit cleans one closed room. Purifiers do not pull air around corners, and an open door to an unfiltered room behaves like an open window. A multi-room home needs a unit per room you actually use.

How long before I notice a difference?

A purifier works by turning a room’s air over repeatedly rather than cleaning it in one pass. Our guarantee is built around one hour in a closed room, which is a fair test of whether it is doing anything at all.

Do I need to run it all day?

It works best running continuously in a closed room. Running it in short bursts means the room reloads with unfiltered air between sessions.

Can I wash the HEPA filter?

Never. Water destroys the fibre structure that does the filtering. The pre-filter layer catches hair and larger dust and can be vacuumed; the H13 layer is a replacement part.

How often will I need to replace the filter?

It depends entirely on what you are asking it to clean — a car in Delhi traffic loads a filter far faster than a bedroom in a coastal town. Replacement intervals and pricing are published with the full specification sheet at launch.

Does it remove smells, or cooking odour?

No, and we will not claim it does. A HEPA filter is a particle filter. Odour molecules and VOCs need a gas-phase medium such as activated carbon.

Does it produce ozone?

Vayoo units filter air mechanically through H13 media. Buying guides routinely warn against ioniser-based purifiers because ionisers can emit ozone — worth checking on any product you are comparing.

What is the AQI meter on the R-10 for?

It shows the air quality in the room, on the unit. It is why the R-10 carries our stronger guarantee: you do not have to believe the claim, you can watch the number.
