What an H13 filter catches

H13 is graded to capture 99.95% of particles at 0.3 microns — the hardest size to trap. Everything below is at or above that size range.

PM2.5 and PM10

the fine particulate that drives most of India’s AQI readings

Vehicle exhaust soot

black carbon from the diesel in front of you

Road dust

the construction and kerbside dust that gets pulled in at every light

Pollen

seasonal allergens, at the size range HEPA is graded for

Particle filtration only. A HEPA filter is not a gas filter — it does not claim to remove odour molecules, VOCs or carbon monoxide, and neither do we.

FAQ

Questions about the Vayoo C-10

Does a car air purifier actually do anything?

In a sealed cabin, yes — a car is a small volume, which is the easiest case for a filter. What it cannot do is help with the window down, because you are then replacing the cabin air faster than any filter can clean it.

Where does it sit?

Anywhere it can draw air freely and will not slide. A cup holder is the usual answer. Keep a few inches clear around the intake.

Will it drain the battery?

It runs from the car’s USB supply. Exact power draw is published with the full specification sheet at launch.
