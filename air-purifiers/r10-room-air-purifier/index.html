What an H13 filter catches

H13 is graded to capture 99.95% of particles at 0.3 microns — the hardest size to trap. Everything below is at or above that size range.

PM2.5 and PM10

the fine particulate that drives most of India’s AQI readings

Smoke and soot

crop-burning haze, cooking smoke, incense, Diwali fallout

Dust and dust-mite debris

a common asthma and rhinitis trigger

Pollen and pet dander

seasonal allergens and airborne skin flakes

Mould spores

released in damp and monsoon conditions

Particle filtration only. A HEPA filter is not a gas filter — it does not claim to remove odour molecules, VOCs or carbon monoxide, and neither do we.

FAQ

Questions about the Vayoo R-10

What does the AQI meter actually show?

The air quality in the room, on the unit itself. It is the reason our guarantee can be what it is: you do not have to believe us, you can watch the number.

Is 350–400 sq.ft. my whole flat?

No. It is one closed room. An open-plan living-dining space usually measures past 400 sq.ft., and the honest answer there is two units.

How long before I see a difference?

A purifier does not clean a room instantly — it works by turning the air over repeatedly. Our guarantee is built around an hour in a closed room, which is a fair test of whether it is doing anything at all.
